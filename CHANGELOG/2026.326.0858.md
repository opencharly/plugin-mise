# 2026.326.0858 — refactor(builder)!: serve every `mise` leg through the one shared builder dispatch

## Summary

plugin-mise now serves every builder leg through the ONE shared dispatch (opencharly/sdk#608): `OpResolve`, `OpCollectContext` and `OpReverse` go to `sdk.InvokeBuilder`, the same for every builder, and the three hand-rolled helpers it carried are gone (R2). The builder plugin's teardown now records the tools it installed and the manifest it removed, which is the parity the sdk#613 contract requires and what plugin-mise#21 reported.

`TestInvokeResolve_ReplyShape` pins the resolve reply to `kit.BuilderResolve("mise", in)`; `TestInvoke_DispatchesTheDeployTimeBuilderOps` pins collect-context and reverse. The test also calls `provider.Invoke` instead of the removed `invokeResolve` helper, and its request fixture now names the op.
